About MTT

Bihar's Own Cab Service

Mithila Tour and Travels is run by Adarsh Raman, from Mithila Dhaba on Bypass Road, Muzaffarpur — but our cabs run across the whole of Bihar. The dhaba doubles as a pit stop for our cabs — so a cab is never far when you need one.

We know the local routes of Bihar properly: the four-lane to Patna, the Darbhanga road, the Raxaul border run. From Patna to Purniya, Darbhanga to Gaya — every town in Bihar is on our map. Landing at Patna, Gaya or Darbhanga airport? We pick you up from arrivals — just share your flight number. Whether it is an airport drop at 4 in the morning or a family trip to Gaya, the driver knows the road before you sit in.

Our fleet is small on purpose — four well-kept cabs, every one checked before a trip. No aggregator commission, no surge games: the rate we tell you on WhatsApp is the rate you pay.

Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers, before you book.

Do you pick up from airports?

Yes — from Patna (PAT), Gaya (GAY) and Darbhanga (DBR) airports. Share your flight number on WhatsApp and the cab will wait at arrivals, even if your flight is delayed.

How can I book a cab?

Tap the 'WhatsApp Now' or 'Call Now' button, or fill the short booking form at the top of this page. It opens WhatsApp with your trip already written — just send it, and we confirm your cab and fare.

Are toll taxes included in the fare?

No. Toll taxes and parking fees (if any on your route) are extra and paid by the customer directly during the journey. We tell you about them before the trip.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Bihar — from any airport or major city (Patna, Muzaffarpur, Gaya, Darbhanga) to any town in the state. We don’t run outside Bihar yet.

What type of cars do you have?

Four types: Mini, Sedan, SUV and Traveller. Pick by your group size and luggage — the booking form lets you choose.

The fine print
